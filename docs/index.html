About Maresono

Maresono (mare + suono — sea + sound) is a generative ocean sound engine that learns from real ocean drum recordings and creates infinite, never-repeating soundscapes.

How it works: Each model is built by analyzing the spectral fingerprint of a real handcrafted ocean drum. The engine extracts the frequency shape, amplitude dynamics, and timbral variation from the recording, then resynthesizes new audio by shaping noise through learned FIR filters — producing sound that is spectrally identical to the real instrument but infinitely generatable.

Philosophy: Rather than simulating physics or looping samples, Maresono takes an analysis-resynthesis approach inspired by how our ears perceive the sea — layers of spectral texture, slow swells, and micro-variations that never quite repeat. The result is a meditative, always-evolving ocean that lives somewhere between the real and the imagined.

Features: Multi-layer envelopes (slow swells modulating individual waves), three-way spectral crossfade (approaching waves are brighter, receding waves are thinner), perceptual corrections for natural warmth, and generative infinite playback with seamless crossfading between segments.
